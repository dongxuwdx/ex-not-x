社区准则Guidelines

在这里，温柔是默认值

Kindness is the default here

Be kind

  1. 先听，再说Listen first

    每一条心事背后都是一个真实的人。回复之前，先读完。Every post is a real person. Read it all before you reply.

  2. 尊重“只想被安慰”Respect “comfort, not advice”

    看到「求安慰」标签时，请不要急着给建议。When a post is marked Needs support, don't rush to give advice.

  3. 不评判任何一种选择No judging anyone's choice

    坚持、离开、暧昧、单恋，都没有标准答案。Staying, leaving, undefined, one-sided — none of them is the wrong answer.

  4. 保护 TA 的隐私Protect their privacy

    不要发布真实姓名、照片、学校或任何能认出别人的信息。No real names, photos, schools or anything that could identify someone else.

  5. 语言不是门槛Language is not a barrier

    中文或 English 都可以，翻译按钮会帮你。不要因为语言嘲笑任何人。Write in either language — the Translate button helps. Never mock anyone for theirs.

我们的故事Our story

Ex not X 是一个给留学生的情感社区：隔着时差的心事，在这里总能找到一个醒着的人。A community for students abroad — whatever your time zone, someone here is awake.

隐私Privacy

这是一个本地原型：投票、帖子、测评与日记只保存在这台设备的浏览器里，不会上传到任何地方。This is a local prototype: votes, posts, quizzes and your journal stay in this browser only.

如果你正处于危险之中，请立刻拨打当地紧急电话。If you are in danger, call your local emergency number now.
